# Revision 10: optical detail by size

The lettering layer and placement remain from revision 09.

Large artwork uses three different white pinpoint core radii (8, 5.5 and 4
units on a 1280-unit canvas), compact near-core flares of different sizes,
and long tapered rays that fade continuously to the canvas boundaries.
Faint narrow optical bloom keeps the rays photographic rather than blunt
plus signs. The letter clearance mask keeps the light behind the letters.

A deterministic sparse field of 65 tiny stars occupies transparent negative
space. Their radii are 0.65–1.55 master pixels and opacity 0.18–0.58; they
are intentionally not enlarged to survive downscaling. The field has no
opaque background.

The approved 16px and 32px star cores are retained, but their long tails
are removed. A compact small-size master supplies 48/49px and 64px exports.
Small exports omit the tiny star field too. PNG and ICO exports use these
optical-size variants. Larger PNGs use source.svg with the full detail.

Sources: source.svg, source-16.svg, source-32.svg, source-small.svg.
The letter/mask PNG layers remain unchanged beside the standalone SVGs.
