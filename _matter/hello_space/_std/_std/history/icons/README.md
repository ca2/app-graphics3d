# Hello Space icon history

Retained source artwork and previews. Dates use America/Sao_Paulo.
The active application icon lives in `../../main/`; these historical files
are for reference. Preserve existing version folders when changing the icon.

| Version | Design | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Original](00-original-hi/source.png) | Original flat hi! application icon | Retained repository baseline |
| [01](2026-10-27-01-pink-orbit/source.png) | White/blue 3D hi!, pink orbit, dark space background | Superseded: requested blue palette and transparency |
| [02](2026-10-27-02-blue-transparent-front-orbit/source.png) | White/blue 3D hi!, cyan front orbit, transparent background | Superseded: bright orbit crosses letters and reduces small-size legibility |

The 3D versions were generated using the built-in image_gen tool. Previews
at 32 and 256 pixels accompany each full-resolution source.

Previous revision: [03](2026-10-27-03-blue-rear-orbit/source.png), richer blue orbit entirely behind the lettering, with uninterrupted white faces for legibility at small sizes.

Revision 03 was generated with image_gen using revision 02 as reference. Prompt: preserve the 3D lettering and transparency; deepen and thin the blue orbit, remove white glare, and place the entire ring behind all letters.


Previous revision: [04](2026-10-27-04-continuous-ellipse/source.png). Adjusted the orbit so the visible arcs follow one continuous tilted ellipse behind the lettering.

Generated with image_gen using revision 03 as reference. Prompt: preserve lettering, palette and transparency; make all visible orbital segments parts of one closed planar ellipse, with continuous tangents and consistent thickness, entirely occluded by the letters where they overlap.


Previous revision: [05](2026-10-27-05-no-orbit-full-square/source.png). User selected the lettering-only version, without an orbit, enlarged to occupy most of the square. Transparent margins are about 4% along the longest dimension.

Lettering extracted using image_gen: remove the entire orbit; preserve the existing 3D white hi! letters, blue sides, perspective and alpha transparency. Standard icon export trims empty margins and fits the artwork within 1180×1180 on a 1280×1280 transparent canvas.


Previous revision: [06](2026-10-27-06-glowing-stars/source.png). Large blue-and-white 3D hi! with three glowing white stars and blue halos in the transparent background. No orbit.

Generated using image_gen. Prompt: large friendly chunky lowercase 3D hi!, glossy white faces and rich blue extrusion, filling most of a transparent square; three small glowing white four-point stars with blue halos around the letters, no overlap on faces, no orbit or background tile.


Previous revision: [07](2026-10-27-07-photographic-stars/source.png). Photographic white pinpoint stars with thin cross-shaped diffraction spikes and faint cool blue glow. The rounded hi! keeps its sculptural style and the background remains transparent.

Both image_gen passes and prompts are retained in [revision 07 notes](2026-10-27-07-photographic-stars/design.md), including the discarded broad-halo draft.


[Revision 08 draft](2026-10-27-08-long-rays-draft/source.png): larger cross rays from revision 06. Not installed because star-to-letter gaps and canvas margins are insufficient. It was retained as an uninstalled draft.


Previous revision: [09](2026-10-27-09-vector-photographic-stars/source.png), [editable SVG](2026-10-27-09-vector-photographic-stars/source.svg). Revision 06 lettering with independently placed photographic vector stars, long cross rays reaching the edges, clear gaps, staggered positions, and small-size optical variants. [Design notes](2026-10-27-09-vector-photographic-stars/design.md).


Previous revision: [10](2026-10-27-10-varied-cores-star-dust/source.png), [editable SVG](2026-10-27-10-varied-cores-star-dust/source.svg). Varied bright cores, very long tapered photographic glow rays and faint tiny background stars in large artwork. Small exports retain revision 09 cross shapes, omitting long tails and fine star dust. [Design notes](2026-10-27-10-varied-cores-star-dust/design.md).


Current revision: [11](2026-10-27-11-inward-stars-unified-resize/source.png), [editable SVG](2026-10-27-11-inward-stars-unified-resize/source.svg). Main star centers moved modestly inward, upper glow lines separated, long gradients kept below core brightness, and tiny background stars strengthened for approximately 64px and larger. All smaller images are direct resizes of one master; no optical variants. [Design notes](2026-10-27-11-inward-stars-unified-resize/design.md).
