# Revision 09: vector photographic stars

Uses revision 06 lettering pixels. The main opaque letter component was
isolated from the three star components, retaining antialiased edges, then
fitted within 1000×820 on the 1280×1280 canvas to make room for separate stars.
The lettering layer is preserved beside the standalone, embedded-image SVG.

Star centers are (170,180), (850,145), and (1150,1160): the right-hand stars
are deliberately staggered. Bright tapered white spikes, small white cores,
circular blue bloom and faint edge-reaching rays provide photographic light
effects without sculptural star bodies. A clearance mask keeps the long rays
from touching or covering letter silhouettes.

The master stays alpha-transparent. The 16px and 32px SVG exports give the
bright cross rays a minimum visible width; these optical-size variants also
supply the corresponding ICO frames. Previews at 16,32,64,256 accompany it.

Authored as vector layers with the user's explicit approval after image edits
repeatedly timed out or produced insufficient spacing. Earlier versions remain
in the parent history folder. Main source: source.svg; small variants:
source-16.svg and source-32.svg.
